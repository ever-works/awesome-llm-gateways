## Overview

APIClaw is an aggregator-type OpenAI-compatible gateway routing multiple model providers on a single key.

## Features

- Aggregator-type gateway
- OpenAI-compatible
- Routes Anthropic, OpenAI, Gemini, DeepSeek and other providers
- One key for multiple providers
- Paid plans billed monthly in crypto
- Crypto payment accepted

## Pricing

Paid service with monthly crypto billing. Prices change frequently; verify on-site.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted aggregator service.
