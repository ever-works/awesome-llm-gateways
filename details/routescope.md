## Overview

RouteScope is an aggregator-type unified gateway mapping 100+ models to OpenAI/Claude/Gemini-compatible APIs.

## Features

- Aggregator-type gateway
- Maps 100+ models to OpenAI/Claude/Gemini-compatible APIs
- Pay-as-you-go credits
- Card and Crypto payment accepted

## Pricing

Paid service with pay-as-you-go credits. Prices change frequently; verify on-site.
