## Overview

Atlas Cloud is a multi-modal aggregator focused on image and video heavy models including Grok Imagine, Kling, ByteDance, and Vidu.

## Features

- Multi-modal aggregator
- Image/video heavy models (Grok Imagine, Kling, ByteDance, Vidu)
- Public OpenAI-compatible /v1/models endpoint
- Exposes per-token pricing including cache-read
- Card payment accepted

## Pricing

Paid service with card payment. Per-token pricing including cache-read exposed via /v1/models.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted aggregator service.
