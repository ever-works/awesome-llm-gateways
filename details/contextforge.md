## Overview

ContextForge is an IBM registry and proxy that federates MCP servers, A2A agents, and REST or gRPC APIs into one endpoint with central governance, discovery, and observability.

## Features

- Federates MCP servers, A2A agents, and REST or gRPC APIs into one endpoint
- Central governance
- Discovery
- Observability
