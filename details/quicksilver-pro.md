## Overview

QuickSilver Pro is an aggregator-type OpenAI-compatible gateway providing one key for frontier and open models including Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, and GLM.

## Features

- Aggregator-type gateway
- OpenAI-compatible
- One key for frontier and open models (Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Qwen, Kimi, GLM)
- Pay-as-you-go pricing
- Public pricing JSON at /pricing.json
- Operated by MachineFi Labs (claimed)
- Card payment accepted

## Pricing

Paid service with pay-as-you-go pricing. Card payment accepted. Prices change frequently; verify on-site.
