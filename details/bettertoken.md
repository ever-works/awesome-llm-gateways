## Overview

BetterToken is an aggregator-type OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible gateway for multiple model providers.

## Features

- Aggregator-type gateway
- OpenAI- and Anthropic-compatible (chat/completions, responses, messages)
- Models: Claude, GPT, Gemini, DeepSeek, Kimi, GLM, MiniMax and Qwen
- Prepaid pay-as-you-go pricing
- Public pricing JSON at /api/pricing with each model's access-group ratio applied
- Card payment accepted

## Pricing

Paid service with prepaid pay-as-you-go pricing. Card payment accepted. Prices change frequently; verify on-site.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted aggregator service.
