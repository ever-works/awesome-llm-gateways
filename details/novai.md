## Overview

NovAI is an aggregator-type OpenAI-compatible aggregator for Chinese frontier models plus image and video generation, all accessible through a single /v1 endpoint.

## Features

- Aggregator-type OpenAI-compatible aggregator
- Chinese frontier models: DeepSeek, Qwen, GLM, Kimi, MiniMax, Doubao, Hunyuan
- Image and video generation on one /v1 endpoint
- Public model list at /v1/models
- Per-token prices on the pricing page
- Card and Crypto payment accepted

## Pricing

Paid service with Card/Crypto payment. Prices change frequently; verify on-site.

## Deployment

Cloud-hosted aggregator service.
